When Covid-19 hit Ireland, my parents were running a catering business and a few cafés. Like many others, they were hit hard by the intermittent closures and were looking for ways to streamline the business and cut costs.

Ingredients are a significant share of any hospitality business's expenses. My parents were spending hours each week creating ingredient lists, reviewing price changes (which happened all the time) and updating recipes, using a mix of spreadsheets and paper.

They asked if I could build something to help, so I started working on Prepsheets. I built the first MVP over the summer of 2020, and within six months of using it they had saved over **€100,000** in ingredient costs.

When my parents showed it to friends in the industry, they wanted to use it too. Over my gap year I kept developing the platform while my parents sold it to other businesses.

I stepped away in August 2021 to focus on my studies, but my parents still run the business. They now have a team of 4 people and over 30 businesses using the platform. You can see the website [here](https://prepsheets.com/).

## Technical details

The frontend is built with React and TypeScript, and the backend runs on Firebase. Key features:

- **Recipe management**: Create recipes, add ingredients, instructions and images.
- **Ingredient management**: Add ingredients and assign them to recipes, along with suppliers and prices.
- **Cost tracking**: See how much each recipe and each ingredient costs.
- **Price tracking**: See how ingredient prices have changed over time.
- **Supplier management**: The system maintains a database of suppliers and the allergen and nutritional information for their products, so users always have up-to-date information.
