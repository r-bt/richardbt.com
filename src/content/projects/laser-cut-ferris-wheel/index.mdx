I designed this ferris wheel for a weekly How to Make Almost Anything (HTMAA) (6.9020) assignment. It features parametric design, [living hinges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_hinge) and wooden joints. 

Every piece was designed in Fusion 360, then laser cut and assembled. All joints are press-fit with no glue used anywhere.
