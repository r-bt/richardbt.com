![Wall posts sketched and revolved](./ship-wall-posts.png)

## 3D printing

I used an Ultimaker S5 to 3D print the pirate ship which has a dual-extruder so I could use PVA for water-soluble supports.

![Pirate ship sliced in Cura](./slicing-pirate-ship.jpg)
*Slicing pirate ship in Cura*

Unfortunately I don't have any pictures of the boat with all the supports, however it took 5 days in a bath of water for everything to dissolve. Once it was in the water, I also realised it doesn't actually work as a boat: it sinks. I'm not too disappointed, since I didn't design it to float, but I'd love to try making a boat that does.
