While at MIT I as a TA for [ES.100](http://student.mit.edu/catalog/search.cgi?search=ES.100) - Introduction to Maker Skills. In 2023, Alayah Hines and I got funding to run an LED neon sign workshop for this class. To test the process I made a sign in the shape of Appa from the Avatar: The Last Airbender.
